# Figure provenance

The PNGs are original illustrations for this article.

- `control-points.png` visualizes the article's observation–decision–execution loop and its three proposed interventions.
- `pear-effort.png` is an original chart of the numbers reported in [PEAR v2, section 4.6 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18562v2). It plots mean thinking tokens per round (0.86k low, 3.23k xhigh) and task success (21.9% low, 74.4% xhigh) for the BF16 LIBERO-only checkpoint. Low values average two evaluations. The chart reproduces factual measurements without reusing the paper's artwork.
- `matched-rollouts.png` illustrates the proposed experiment. It presents the procedure and contains no measured data.

- `reasoning-control-levels.png` compares [GPT-5 system routing](https://cdn.openai.com/gpt-5-system-card.pdf), [request-level effort](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning), and [Claude adaptive thinking](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/adaptive-thinking). It is a conceptual comparison across systems.
- `training-mechanisms.png` summarizes the training objectives described by [Qwen3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1#S4.SS3), [Kimi k1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12599v1#S2.SS4), [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19969v1#S5.SS1.SSS4), and [R-4B](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21113v2#S3). It presents capabilities and supervision mechanisms without comparing benchmark scores.
- `robotics-architectures.png` abstracts the functional handoffs in [Gemini Robotics 1.5, sections 3.3 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.03342v3) and [PEAR v2, section 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18562v2). The return arrows denote execution feedback.
- `training-evaluation-flow.png` illustrates the experiment proposed in the article, with stages from matched branches through held-out evaluation. It contains no measured data.
