# Figure provenance

The three PNGs are original illustrations for this article.

- `control-points.png` visualizes the article's observation–decision–execution loop and its three proposed interventions.
- `pear-effort.png` is an original chart of the numbers reported in [PEAR v2, section 4.6 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18562v2). It plots mean thinking tokens per round (0.86k low, 3.23k xhigh) and task success (21.9% low, 74.4% xhigh) for the BF16 LIBERO-only checkpoint. Low values average two evaluations. The chart reproduces factual measurements without reusing the paper's artwork.
- `matched-rollouts.png` illustrates the proposed experiment. It presents the procedure and contains no measured data.
